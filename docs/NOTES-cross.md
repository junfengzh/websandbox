## Decisions

- **427 (Turing Award)**: kept with the intent fixed to "the university **in Massachusetts** that
  has the most Turing Award winners" (and "Turning" -> "Turing"). Our 2022-05 wiki favours Stanford
  (29) over MIT (26) and Stanford is not on the map; in Massachusetts MIT (26) clearly beats Harvard
  (14), so the reference MIT holds.
- **267 (Stephen King's hometown)**: Bangor. The wiki article says born in Portland, raised in
  Durham, and calls Bangor "his home town"; WebArena's 1h 23min also fits Bangor. Lite answer:
  Acadia + 78 min (10 %); Portland (3h 36min) fails.
- 425: "the longest bridge" -> "the longest suspension bridge" in the Western Hemisphere (by length it
  is the Lake Pontchartrain Causeway, not on the map; the Mackinac article states the suspension claim).
- 553: "the news related subreddits" -> "the news subreddit" (news / worldnews / UpliftingNews).
- 558: "after 2010" -> "since 2010" (the reference includes Inception, 2010).
- 671-675: "games subreddit" -> "gaming subreddit" (no /f/games; as tasks/forum 644).
- 681-683: the post no longer has to contain the phrase "gan implementation" / "chatgpt" /
  "metaseq"; linking the repo is what the intent asks. Repo links count with any host except
  github.com (the code site's host varies per tenant).
- Map numbers re-derived on our engines: 97 913 km (WebArena 914), 265 422 km (457: different Acadia
  end point), 267 1h 18min, 268 bike 10h 13min. Place tasks accept a list of OSM objects (WebArena
  accepted any page whose text had the word, e.g. any place in Somerset County).
- 738 (76ers in the 70s): the Spectrum (3601 South Broad Street) is demolished; the arena next to
  it has the same address, so the route must end within 800 m with "South Broad Street" (or the
  arena name) in To. 739 (Yankees in the 80s): old Yankee Stadium, demolished, next to the new one.
- 791 (cars forum): unachievable (no such forum), answer "N/A" for the LLM judge, as WebArena.

## Site fixes / findings

- map geocoder: "Allentown, PA" ranked the Allentown neighbourhood of Pittsburgh (next to the map
  view) above the city of Allentown; a qualified settlement name now weakens the view-proximity
  bonus (sites/map/lib/geocode.js; check_places top hits otherwise unchanged; test added).
- kit: `--loopback-port` for `--bind rl`, so all sites can share port 80 on their 127.10.<i>.<k>
  addresses (tasks/tools/sites.sh). With `--bind ports` all sites are on 127.0.0.1 and share the
  `wsb_sid` cookie (cookies ignore ports): cross-site browsing needs `--bind rl`.
- gitlab project pages whose README links external images (badges) never fire `load` without
  internet (browse.js `goto` timed out after 15 s on auth0/angular-storage; the page itself is
  served in ms and usable). Rollout harnesses should not wait for `load` on such pages, or block
  external requests so they fail fast.
